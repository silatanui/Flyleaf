The first sheet, before the verdict.

A flyleaf is the blank page at the front of a book. Flyleaf is that first look at a draft: paste a paragraph, or upload a PDF and download the marked pages. I am Tanui Kipngetich Sila, and anyone can use this for free.

The name

A page you meet before the story.

The folded corner in the mark is that flyleaf. The short rust line is a reader's note, the kind someone writes in the margin before they decide what the page means.

How it works

Three steps, then the page is yours.

  1. 01

    Bring a draft

    Paste English prose, or upload a text-based PDF or Word file.

  2. 02

    Read the highlights

    Pasted text opens beside your draft. A PDF opens on its own page, with marks on the original lines.

  3. 03

    Keep the review

    Print or download the report. It opens with a cover and an overview, then every original page.

Two ways in

A paragraph beside you, or a PDF you can keep.

Pasted text

The draft stays on the left. Highlights, the flagged share, and the likelihood sit on the right, so you can read both at once.

Uploaded PDF

Flyleaf opens the same report you can download: a cover, the percentage, then the original pages with cyan and purple marks.

A file to download

The file and the preview are the same report. Cyan is AI-like. Purple is uncertain. The header and footer stay on every page.

How to read a score

Three numbers, each with a small job.

Flagged share

How much of the scored writing sits in passages the model marks as AI-like. A mixed draft should not collapse into one color.

Likelihood

The average chance across those passages. It is a reading of the draft, useful beside the highlights, not instead of them.

Confidence

Lower on short text, on mixed sections, and on writing that may not be English. Under 300 words, treat the result as a sketch.

Who it is for

A second pair of eyes, with no login.

Students

Teachers

Walk into a conversation with the highlighted page, rather than with a single percentage.

Writers

Look at a passage you edited with a model, and keep the PDF if you want a record of that look.

About this site

Built as a portfolio, offered for free.

My name is Tanui Kipngetich Sila. Flyleaf is a project I made to show a complete writing-review tool: a public homepage, a checker, and a downloadable PDF report.

There is no account and no fee. A percentage is a reason to reread a passage. It does not decide that someone used a chatbot, and it does not grade the work.
