How to read a score

Two shares, and the uncertainty inside them.

Suspicious share

Lines classified as AI-like, plus lines that were inconclusive. That is the number on the gauge, from 0 to 100.

Human-like share

The rest of the scored writing. A mixed draft should not collapse into one color. Read the highlights beside the number.

Confidence

Lower on short text, on mixed sections, and on writing that may not be English. Under 300 words, treat the result as a sketch.

About this site

Built as a portfolio, offered for free.

The name comes from the flyleaf, the blank page at the front of a book. Flyleaf is that first look at a draft.

My name is Tanui Kipngetich Sila. I made this to show a complete writing-review tool: a public homepage, a checker, and a downloadable PDF report.

There is no account and no fee. A percentage is a reason to reread a passage. It does not decide that someone used a chatbot, and it does not grade the work.
