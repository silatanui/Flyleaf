Privacy

What Flyleaf keeps

Flyleaf is a writing review by Tanui Kipngetich Sila. This note describes the information the site handles. It is not a promise that a score is a finding about a person.

An account

If you create an account, Flyleaf stores your name, email, and a hash of your password. If you continue with Google, Google confirms the email and Flyleaf stores a link to that Google account. Flyleaf does not receive your Google password.

A check

Text you paste is reviewed in the session so the highlights can be shown. It is not added to your document list. A PDF that produces a report can be kept with your account, including the file name, the date, the score summary, and the report file. You can delete a kept report from the dashboard.

Where it is stored

Account details and kept reports are stored on the computer that runs Flyleaf. A session cookie remembers that you are signed in on that browser. Flyleaf does not sell this information.

How long it stays

A kept report stays until you delete it or the account is removed. A check that is not kept lasts only while Flyleaf is running. You can sign out at any time.

A question

Write to silatanuikipngetich@gmail.com if you want a copy of what an account holds, or if you want that account removed.
