How to use Flyleaf

Flyleaf is a free English writing review by Tanui Kipngetich Sila. Use this page for a short guide. For anything else, send a message through the contact form on my site.

AI Detector

Paste a draft or upload a text-based PDF or Word file. Flyleaf marks the share classified as AI-like and keeps inconclusive lines separate. The percentage is an estimate. It is not a finding of misconduct.

AI Humanizer

Humanize returns a plainer draft for you to review. The meaning should stay. A later check can still mark the result. Flyleaf does not promise a lower score.

Plagiarism Checker

Similarity looks for close matches on public web pages found for the draft, then builds a short report. It does not search publication libraries or student-paper databases. A match is a reason to cite or rewrite.

Accounts and PDF reports

Paste checks stay on the page. A PDF that produces a report can be kept with your account after you sign in. The free plan includes 5 kept PDF reports each day unless you are an admin on this computer.
