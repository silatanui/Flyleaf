Terms

How to use Flyleaf

Flyleaf is a free writing review by Tanui Kipngetich Sila. Using the site means you accept the limits below.

The score is an estimate

The headline is the share of the draft classified as AI-like. Inconclusive lines are kept separate. Highlights are a reason to reread a passage. They are not a finding of misconduct, they do not prove who wrote the text, and they do not grade the work. Do not use a percentage as the only basis for a penalty.

What you may upload

Upload only text you have a right to review. English prose works best. A short passage, under 300 words, is a rough reading, not a document finding.

The free plan

Checking text on the homepage has no daily cap. The free plan includes 5 PDF reports kept with an account each day. More plans may be added later. Until then, that is the allowance.

A rewrite

Humanize produces another draft for you to review. It does not promise a lower AI-like number, and it is not a way to hide the origin of a text. Read the result before you use it.

The service

Flyleaf is a portfolio project. It can be unavailable, and a reading can change if the model or the text changes. Reports you keep stay on the computer that runs the site until you delete them.
